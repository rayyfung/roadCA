# roadCA

This paper presents a structural analysis of the California road networks, focusing on the intricate interconnections and community formations within this extensive transportation system. By utilizing methodologies from network theory, this paper examines the topology of the road network, highlighting key metrics such as centrality, clustering coefficients and modularity. The analysis reveals different communities within the network, driven by many factors that significantly influence traffic patterns and accessibility. This paper ultimately contributes to an in-depth discourse on the structure of the network beneath the California Road Network and provides the reader with more insights and understandings regarding network analysis on transportation roads. Only the necessary figures and the codes are committed to this repo and the paper is submitted through the assignment submission system.
